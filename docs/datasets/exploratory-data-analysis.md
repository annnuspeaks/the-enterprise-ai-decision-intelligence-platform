## 2.1.5.3 Business Insights

The exploratory data analysis reveals several meaningful business patterns within the processed customer dataset.

### Customer Purchasing Behavior

- Most customers have placed only a small number of orders, indicating that repeat purchasing is relatively limited.
- A small group of customers contributes disproportionately to higher purchase frequencies, suggesting the presence of valuable repeat customers.

### Customer Spending Patterns

- Customer spending is positively associated with average order value.
- Customers with higher average order values generally contribute more to overall revenue.

### Review Score Analysis

- Customer review scores show very little linear relationship with spending or purchase frequency.
- This suggests that additional business factors, such as delivery experience, product quality, or customer service, may influence customer satisfaction.

### Order Distribution

- The purchase distribution is highly right-skewed.
- Most customers make relatively few purchases, while only a small percentage become frequent buyers.

### Outlier Analysis

- Several high-value customers exist with significantly larger purchase activity.
- These customers should be treated as important business segments rather than removed as data anomalies.

### Business Opportunities

- Increase customer retention through loyalty programs and personalized marketing.
- Target repeat customers with exclusive offers to maximize customer lifetime value.
- Analyze customer satisfaction drivers beyond purchase amount to improve overall experience.
- Focus marketing efforts on converting one-time customers into repeat buyers.
